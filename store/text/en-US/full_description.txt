Klas is a lesson book for a private tutor: your schedule, your students, who has paid and who owes — worked out for you, on your phone.

YOUR TEACHING DAY AT A GLANCE
• Day, week and month views of every lesson
• See what's taught, what's on now and who's next
• Book a lesson once or repeat it every week or two
• An overlapping time is refused, so you're never double-booked
• Mark a lesson taught with a swipe — or let Klas do it once the lesson ends

MONEY WITHOUT THE SPREADSHEET
• An hourly price per student and subject, with a price history
• Changing a price never rewrites lessons already taught
• Record payments; balances, debts and credit are worked out for you
• Sort students by who owes the most

GROUPS
• Book one lesson for the whole group at the group's own price
• Billing stays per student — including "this one didn't come"

HOMEWORK THAT FINDS THE NEXT LESSON
• Set homework for a student or a group in a few words
• It attaches to their next lesson on its own
• Share it with the student in one tap, tick it off when it's done

REPORTS
• Income for a month, a year or all time, compared with the period before
• Breakdowns by month, subject, student and group, individual vs group
• Outstanding debts in one list
• Export to CSV for a spreadsheet or your accountant

REMINDERS AND CALENDAR
• A notification before each lesson, on time
• Optional morning agenda, evening list of unmarked lessons and weekly debt summary
• Optional sync to your phone's calendar, with email invitations for students

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no internet needed
• Your data stays on your phone — Klas has no servers to send it to
• Full backup to a file you keep, and restore on a new phone
• No ads and no trackers

Made for a tutor who works alone. 25 languages and over 150 currencies. Light and dark themes.

Klas is open source under the GNU GPL v3.
