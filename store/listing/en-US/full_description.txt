Klas is the lesson book for private tutors and teachers. Your tutoring schedule, students, homework and money — who has paid, who owes and what you earned this month — in one app that works offline and never asks you to sign up.

Less time on spreadsheets and chasing payments. More time teaching.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
Open Klas and see the lesson on now, who's next, which lessons still need marking and who owes you. Swipe a lesson to mark it taught, or let Klas mark lessons automatically once they end.

A SCHEDULE THAT KEEPS UP WITH YOU
• Day, week and month views
• Book a lesson once, every week or every two weeks
• Reschedule or cancel in a tap, and undo it if you change your mind
• Overlapping bookings are refused, so you're never double-booked

KNOW WHO OWES — WITHOUT DOING THE MATH
• An hourly rate per student and subject, with a full rate history
• Raising a rate never rewrites lessons already taught
• Record full, partial or advance payments; balances, debts and credit update on their own
• Sort students by who owes the most
• Send a student or parent a PDF statement of lessons and payments
• Call, message or email a student straight from their profile

GROUP LESSONS, BILLED FAIRLY
• Book one lesson for the whole group at the group's rate
• Track attendance per student: absent and not charged, or absent and charged
• Every student keeps their own balance

HOMEWORK THAT FINDS THE NEXT LESSON
• Set homework for a student or a group in a few words
• It attaches to their next lesson automatically
• Share it in one tap, tick it off when it's done

REPORTS THAT SHOW YOUR BUSINESS GROWING
• Income for a month, a year or all time, compared with the period before
• Breakdowns by month, subject, student and group
• Every outstanding debt in one list
• Export to PDF or CSV for your records or your accountant

NEVER MISS A LESSON
• A reminder before every lesson
• Optional morning agenda, evening list of unmarked lessons and weekly debt summary
• Home-screen widgets: today's lessons with a one-tap tick, plus shortcuts to booking, homework and debts
• Optional sync with your phone's calendar, with email invitations for students

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no sign-up, no internet needed
• Everything stays on your phone — Klas has no servers to send it to
• App lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Names and amounts kept off the lock screen
• Password-protected backups, an automatic weekly copy to a folder or cloud drive you choose, and a preview before you restore
• Free, with no ads, no subscriptions and no trackers

Made for independent tutors and private teachers of any subject — languages, math, music, exam prep and more. 25 languages, over 150 currencies, light and dark themes.

Klas is open source under the GNU GPL v3.
