# Klas — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Klas (Android application ID: `com.seblful.klas`) is a tutoring-management app developed by
**Aliaksei Chymba**. It helps tutors manage students, groups, subjects, lessons, homework, rates,
payments, and outstanding balances.

Privacy contact: [alesha.chimba@gmail.com](mailto:alesha.chimba@gmail.com).

## Information stored and its purpose

Klas stores information you enter or import so it can organize your tutoring work:

- Student names, phone numbers, email addresses, and notes.
- Groups, group membership, subjects, lesson schedules, and attendance.
- Homework text and completion records.
- Hourly rates, rate history, payment records, and information used to calculate balances and reports.
- App preferences, such as language, currency, appearance, and reminder settings.

Klas records payments for bookkeeping; it does not process financial transactions or request payment
card credentials.

## Local storage, accounts, and tracking

Your tutoring database and preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your Android
device. Klas does not require an account or operate a developer-hosted server for your tutoring
records. The developer does not automatically receive a copy of your database.

Klas does not include advertising, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer
does not sell your personal data. Optional transfers and platform diagnostics are described below.

Android automatic app backup is disabled. Klas instead provides a manual backup and restore feature.

## Optional calendar synchronization

Calendar synchronization is off by default. If you enable it and grant calendar permissions, Klas
reads the device's available calendars to find one it can write to and creates or updates lesson
events. It also reads and manages attendees for the events it manages.

Events can contain student names, subject and group names, lesson dates and times, and whether a
lesson is online or in person. Event descriptions are written empty; current versions do not add
homework or student notes to them.

Your device's calendar service can synchronize these events to Google Calendar or another connected
calendar provider. If you separately enable student email invitations, student email addresses are
added as attendees, and the calendar provider can send invitations and reminders. That provider
handles the transferred information under its own privacy policy.

You can disable synchronization and invitations in Klas settings and revoke calendar permissions in
Android settings. Disabling a feature does not necessarily delete events or messages already held by
another service.

## Notifications and permissions

Klas uses notification permission to show lesson reminders and optional agenda or debt summaries.
Notifications can display student names, lesson details, homework, or balances. By default, Klas hides
these details on a secure lock screen and shows only the kind of notification until you unlock the
phone; you can choose to show them in Settings → Privacy & security. Marking a lesson taught from a
notification asks you to unlock first. The Today widget lists lessons on a lock screen only if you
choose so.

Exact-alarm access, where granted, helps deliver timely reminders. Klas also reschedules reminders
after a device restart. These reminder features do not require sending your records to the developer.
You can change reminder settings in Klas and control permissions and lock-screen visibility in Android.

## Backups, exports, and actions you choose

You can export a JSON backup or CSV report to storage you select, including a cloud-storage provider
available through Android's file picker. You can also choose a folder for a weekly backup, which Klas
then writes to on its own until you turn it off. You can also share reports or homework through another
app. Backup files contain tutoring records and can include student contact information and payment
history. Before an import replaces your data, Klas keeps a copy of it in its private storage on this
device, so the import can be undone; the next import replaces that copy.

Contact buttons can open your dialer, email app, or Telegram using a student's phone number or email
address. Klas passes the relevant information to the selected app or service when you choose the
action. Klas does not read your phone's address book.

Recipients, storage providers, calendar providers, and communication services handle information under
their own policies. Choose destinations and recipients carefully. You can protect an exported backup with
a password: Klas then encrypts it (AES-256), and nobody, including the developer, can open it without that
password. Backups saved without a password, weekly backups, and CSV reports are not encrypted; use secure
storage and sharing methods.

## Klas Pro purchases

Klas Pro, the paid tier, is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the purchase, your
payment details and any subscription under the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/u/0/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Klas asks Google Play on your device
which Klas Pro plan your Google account owns and keeps only that answer, in the app's private storage. The
developer does not receive your payment details, and Klas sends nothing about your purchase or your tutoring
records to a developer server.

## Crash diagnostics

Klas saves a local crash log containing crash times, app and Android versions, device model
information, and technical error details. Error details can include information present in an error
message. Older log entries are removed as the log grows. You can view, clear, or manually export the
log from Diagnostics. Klas does not automatically upload this local log to the developer.

Android and Google Play can separately collect crash and performance diagnostics through Android
Vitals, depending on your device settings and Google's policies, and make diagnostic reports
available to the developer. Klas does not use a third-party crash-reporting SDK.

## Retention, deletion, and security

Local tutoring records remain on your device until removed through an available app function or until
you clear Klas's app storage or uninstall it. Archiving a student, group, or homework item retains the
record; it is not deletion. Some records are retained in the app to preserve lesson and billing history.
You can remove the entire local database by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling Klas.

You can clear the local crash log separately in Diagnostics. Exported files, shared messages, and
synced calendar events are separate copies and must be deleted from their destinations. Clearing app
storage or uninstalling Klas does not delete those copies.

Klas relies on Android's app-private storage and operating-system protections. It does not add its own
database encryption. You can turn on an app lock, which asks for your fingerprint, face, or screen lock
through Android when Klas opens; Klas never receives or stores biometric data, only whether the unlock
succeeded. You can also hide Klas's preview in recent apps (below Android 13 this also blocks
screenshots of the app). Protect your device with a screen lock and keep backups secure. Because the
developer does not hold your tutoring database, the developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase it.

## Information about students and minors

Klas is a management tool for tutors, not a student account service. Tutors are responsible for having
the necessary permission or other lawful basis to enter and share information about students,
including minors. Enter only information needed for your tutoring work.
